About SOTA
Regulatory Requirements Management System

Purpose

Our platform assists reviewers by automatically identifying relevant regulatory standards from uploaded device-requirement documents, improving consistency and reducing manual effort.

Most medical device developers spend a significant portion of their time manually verifying and organizing regulatory requirements from lengthy documents across various regulatory sources. Their requirements documents must have the most up-to-date compliances to ensure a smooth review process from a professional, otherwise they risk facing delays in their product development and costly fees to review a resubmitted document.

By leveraging AI, our tool parses through complex requirements documents and extracts all relevant items that will be compared with the most recent metadata retrieved by our webscraper.

Intended Use

This tool is intended to support internal review workflows. It is not a substitute for professional regulatory or compliance judgment.

AI Disclaimer

All AI-generated outputs must be independently reviewed and validated by a qualified professional before being used in any regulatory, compliance, or submission context.
